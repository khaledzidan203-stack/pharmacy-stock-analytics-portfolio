# Assumptions

- Analysis date: 2026-08-26 for the generated synthetic sample.
- Demand window: trailing 90 days.
- Reorder review threshold: less than 14 days of cover.
- Expiry risk horizon: 30 days.
- Slow-moving threshold: more than 120 days of cover with positive sales.
- Zero-sales stock is classified separately as dead stock.
- Retail price is used only as a portfolio value proxy; production accounting should use governed cost data where appropriate.
- No supplier lead time, safety stock, open purchase order, case pack, or promotion data is modeled.
